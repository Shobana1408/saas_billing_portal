\# AI-Powered SaaS Billing \& Analytics Portal



\## Project Overview



The AI-Powered SaaS Billing \& Analytics Portal is a role-based subscription and billing management system developed using Flask, MySQL, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.



The platform is designed for SaaS businesses to manage:



\- Companies

\- Users

\- Subscription plans

\- Billing

\- Invoices

\- Payments

\- Revenue analytics

\- AI-based business insights



The system supports multiple user roles with different permissions and dashboards.



\---



\# Objectives



\- Build a real-world SaaS billing platform

\- Implement role-based access control

\- Manage subscriptions and invoices
